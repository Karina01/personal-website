Hi, I'm Karina.

I'm half British, half Danish. I was raised bilingually in the UK, with frequent trips to family in Denmark. I studied Mathematics and Management at the University of Manchester and spent most of my twenties living and working in London. Now, I live in Copenhagen (Denmark) with my husband and young daughter.

My Dad often says that I have only two speeds: fast or asleep. My default setting is to be on-the-go and packing as much into the day as I can. Honestly, I'm trying to change this; to rest more and move through the world more calmly. I suspect this will be my life's work.

So, what might be worth knowing about me?

My family is my happy place. Being my daughter's mum is one of my favourite things. I feel wildly lucky to have a front-row seat, alongside my best friend, as our daughter grows.

I love to read. Left to my own devices I would probably only read fantasy fiction, but I am part of a wonderful book club that helps broaden my literary horizons.

I have an incurable urge to travel and experience new places. I secretly enjoy the planning process: dreaming about how it should feel, researching different options, curating the trip.

Movement helps me in countless ways. I've struggled with consistency but I know that exercise needs to feel fun for me to stick with it. I enjoy running, bouldering and calisthenics.

Close friendships are a special kind of love, in my experience. I'm fortunate to have some amazing friends to travel through life with.

So that's a bit about me beyond work. If you'd like to see more about what I'm currently working on, click [here](/current-projects/).
